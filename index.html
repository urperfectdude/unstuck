FAQ

The questions everyone asks.

Why do I have to be interviewed?

Because the room is the product. Twenty people is small enough that one wrong person changes the weekend for everyone.

What counts as a “project”?

Anything specific and shippable in three days. A launch, a site, a course, a first version, a pitch, a portfolio, a piece of writing you've been avoiding. “Grow my business” isn't a project.

What if I don't finish?

Most people finish. Everyone who doesn't leaves further along in three days than in the previous three months, with a partner still checking on them.

Where in Goa?

We confirm the villa once the cohort is locked, and share it with everyone who's in. North Goa, easy reach from the airport.

Can I come for one day?

No. The whole thing works because everyone's there for all of it.

What's the refund policy?

Full refund if you cancel more than 14 days out. After that we can transfer your seat but can't refund it. The villa and food are booked on your headcount.
